How it works
From URL to report in three steps
You stay in control of what is tested and how long it takes.



  
1
Find pages
The agent reads the sitemap and follows links to map the site: sections, page count and same-template groups. No tests yet.

  
2
Choose and test
Pick a smart sample, a number of pages or whole sections, with a time estimate. Every page is tested on desktop and mobile.

  
3
Review the evidence
Defects are grouped, ranked by severity and backed by HTTP status, field results and screenshots.





What it checks
Thorough, safe and honest
PASS only with evidence. Anything it cannot verify is labelled as such, never hidden.



  
Links & navigation
Clicks every visible link, checks the real HTTP status, and reports hidden and broken links with the reason.

  
Form validation
Required fields, email and format rules, and realistic values, checked field by field. Forms are never submitted.

  
Broken images
Scrolls to load lazy images, then separates truly broken files from slow, lazy or hidden ones.

  
Mobile experience
Tests every page on a phone-sized screen, including whether the hamburger menu opens and shows its links.

  
Smart page finder
Reads the sitemap, groups pages by section and spots same-template pages, so big sites stay fast to test.

  
Safe by design
Never buys, sends or deletes; blocks state-changing requests; respects robots.txt and slows down when a site rate-limits.
